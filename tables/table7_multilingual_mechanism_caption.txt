Projection (M1) against independent argmax (M0) on five corpora, each on its own primary backbone at $\lambda$ = 0 and its document-disjoint protocol, means over three seeds. M0's illegal rate spans 12.55\% to 31.25\%; M1's is 0\% everywhere by construction. The two net columns are the mechanism. Projection's only lever on a child field is writing $N\!/\!A$, so its repairs land on that class and its damage on the substantive ones: the sign pattern is $N\!/\!A$ positive and substantive negative in five of five corpora. Macro-F1 weights those classes equally, which is where the movement cancels. Whole-row accuracy rises in five of five corpora; the official metric rises in two. The same predictions, scored two ways, do not agree on whether anything improved. \textbf{The metric's exchange rate is not the same in every corpus.} macro-F1 averages over the classes present in gold, and Korean has no Misleading row, so its evidence\_quality divides by one class fewer: a point of one class's F1 is worth 0.1167 there against 0.0875 elsewhere. Cross-corpus scores are therefore not on one scale and are not ranked here.
