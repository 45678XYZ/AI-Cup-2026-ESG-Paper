What enforcing legality costs. Each row contrasts M1 with M0 on the pdf_group protocol: the same probabilities decoded by unconstrained per-field argmax, and projected onto the 17 legal states. Means over 3 seeds; intervals from the study's paired PDF-cluster bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 20260814), with one resample shared by both methods. M1's invalid-tuple rate is 0 in every arm by construction -- its output space is the legal set -- so it is stated here rather than printed as a column of zeros. M0's ranges from 3.68\% to 19.75\%. The cost on the official metric is negative in all 4 untrained arms and within $\pm$0.001 in all 3 arms trained with the structural objective, while the tuple-accuracy gain is positive in all 7. \textbf{These seven contrasts are exploratory.} They were named after the primary analysis, are not a Holm family, and support no claim on their own; bold marks an interval excluding zero, not a corrected verdict.
