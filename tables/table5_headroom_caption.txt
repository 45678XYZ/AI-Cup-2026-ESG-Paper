Where the official score falls short under M1, and what closing each part is worth. Shortfall is the field's weight times its distance from a perfect macro-F1; share is its portion of the total. The last column is what a 0.1 gain on a single class's F1 adds to the weighted total -- the field's weight divided by its class count, times 0.1 -- which orders the fields differently from the shortfall: macro-F1 averages over classes, so a field carrying more of them returns less per class improved. \textbf{Not all of the shortfall is reachable.} Misleading occurs twice in the development set and is never predicted by any arm, so a quarter of evidence\_quality's macro-F1 -- 0.0875 of the weighted total -- is out of reach by construction.
