Anatomy of the 1{,}527 invalid tuples independent argmax (M0) emits over 12{,}000 scored rows -- both protocols, all three seeds. In (a) a row is attributed to the first implication it breaks, so the four counts partition the 1{,}527 exactly. Panel (b) derives the Introduction's motivating figure: restricted to those same invalid rows, each field is scored on its own, so the row still collects its weight times its per-field accuracy, and the four terms sum to 0.443. That is weighted field accuracy on invalid rows, not weighted macro-F1, which has no per-row decomposition.
