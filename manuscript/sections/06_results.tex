\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\paragraph{Controlled decision comparison.}
Table~\ref{tab:main-results} compares decision rules on identical base
probabilities and Test rows. Independent argmax (M0) produces invalid tuples
for 12.6\% of document-disjoint predictions; projection and 17-state decoding
(M1--M6) reduce this rate to zero by construction. The $\pm$ values are sample
standard deviations across the three seeds. Because each seed determines both
fold assignment and training randomness, they describe spread over the whole
split-and-training pipeline, not a confidence interval.

\paragraph{Evaluation targets and robustness.}
Table~\ref{tab:regimes} compares the same-document and document-disjoint
targets. Its gaps, $0.012$--$0.015$, separate estimands rather than estimate
bias.

The C-wF1 result does not replicate under same-document evaluation: M1--M0
remains positive at $+0.002$ $[-0.001,0.005]$ but does not survive Holm
correction ($p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=.739$).

\paragraph{Metric--constraint mismatch.}
Table~\ref{tab:contrasts} reports all 20 paired contrasts. For the
pre-specified official-metric M1--M0 contrast, $\Delta=-0.001$ and the interval
is $[-0.006,0.003]$. With
$p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=1.000$, there is no detectable difference. Post-hoc C-wF1
changes only whether an ancestor-unsupported child prediction receives credit.
It gives $+0.004$ $[0.001,0.007]$ and survives correction with
$p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=.025$. Post-hoc hF gives $\Delta=+0.003$ and corroborates
the direction with $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=.017$; its interval is
$[0.001,0.005]$. However, hF also replaces macro with micro aggregation and
therefore does not isolate consistency.
Pre-specified tuple accuracy corroborates the direction with $+0.035$
$[0.028,0.043]$ and $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=.001$.

The complete family also contains adverse evidence. Compared with projection,
uncalibrated 17-state decoding (M4--M1) lowers tuple accuracy by $-0.006$
$[-0.010,-0.002]$, $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=.028$. Thus validity-preserving
decoding is not uniformly superior. Searching every legal state need not
improve whole-row correctness.
